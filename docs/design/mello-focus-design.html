Mello, one thing at a time

The redesign keeps one job per screen. The app picks your next step and gives you one big button to start it, the way Duolingo puts the next lesson first.

  • 1One filled button per screen, full width, where the thumb rests.
  • 2At most one other action, as a plain text link.
  • 3Mello picks the next step. You don't choose from a list.
  • 4Stats, settings and history wait in Me, one tap away.
  • 5A warning shows only when it blocks the next step.

The main path

Start → do it → done → next. Everything here is clickable.

Onboarding: 4 steps, one decision each. Defaults are picked for you.

Today: one next step and one button. The day switcher changes which step.

Focus: one way back in. Stopping early lives behind a link, in a sheet.

Done: the win, then Continue straight to the next step.

Out of the way, still there

Floating window, widgets, and Me.

Streak: the whole widget opens Mello. Goals: the next goal and one Start.

Me: habits, goals, companion and privacy. A plain list, no competing buttons.
